Pet Information Center

Caring for Every Companion

Each pet species has unique dietary, emotional, and physical needs. Explore our practical care notes for common companions below.

Canine Companion

Dogs (All Breeds)

Loyal, active, and social. Dogs thrive on structured daily routines, engaging walks, brain puzzles, and affectionate praise.

Daily Exercise: 45 - 90 mins
Grooming Needs: Every 4-6 weeks
Dietary Focus: High-protein balance
💡 Keep daily walks mentally stimulating by allowing them time to sniff and explore scents.
Feline Royalty

Cats (Indoor & Outdoor)

Independent yet affectionate. Cats require clean vertical spaces, clean litter boxes, and daily interactive hunting-style play sessions.

Daily Play: 20 - 30 mins
Hydration: Water fountains preferred
Grooming Needs: Regular brush & de-shed
💡 Place scratching posts near resting areas to help keep claws healthy and prevent furniture damage.
Gentle Herbivore

Rabbits & Bunnies

Gentle, inquisitive, and sensitive. Rabbits require unlimited fresh timothy hay, safe bunny-proofed hopping spaces, and calm handling.

Primary Diet: 80% Fresh Timothy Hay
Environment: Quiet & free from drafts
Handling: Support hind legs always
💡 Bunnies have delicate spines; never hold them loosely and always provide unlimited fresh hay.
🦜 Avian Friends

Birds (Parakeets & Canaries)

Highly social and intelligent. Birds need varied perch textures, mentally challenging foraging toys, and fresh vegetable supplements.

Social Time: 1 - 2 hours daily
Dietary Focus: Pellets, seeds & greens
Air Safety: Zero non-stick teflon fumes
💡 Never expose birds to aerosol sprays, scented candles, or overheated non-stick cookware fumes.
🐹 Small Pets

Guinea Pigs & Hamsters

Charming small pocket pets. Guinea pigs need daily Vitamin C supplementation, while hamsters thrive in large solitary enclosures with deep burrowing bedding.

Bedding: Dust-free paper bedding
Nutritional Need: Daily Vitamin C drops
Chew Toys: Natural untreated apple wood
💡 Small rodents' teeth grow continuously; wooden chews and mineral blocks are essential for dental health.

Knowledge Base

Helpful Pet Care Advice

Simple daily habits recommended by our veterinarians to keep your pet energetic, healthy, and smiling.

💧

Fresh Hydration Routine

Ensure water bowls are cleaned daily and replenished with cool, filtered water. Cats love circulating water fountains which encourage them to drink more.

🥗

Portion Control & Nutrition

Measure daily meal portions with a measuring cup rather than free-feeding. Balanced nutrition prevents obesity and supports joint longevity.

🎾

Mental Stimulation

15 minutes of puzzle toys, scent games, or obedience games per day tires out high-energy dogs and reduces anxiety far better than exercise alone.

🦷

Routine Dental Care

Dental hygiene directly affects your pet's heart and kidney health. Use pet-safe enzymatic toothpaste and veterinarian-approved dental chews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your pet's first visit.

For the safety of all guests, dogs must have up-to-date Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccines. Cats must have Rabies and FVRCP. We kindly ask you to bring digital or paper vaccination records during your first visit.

We believe in low-stress handling! Anxious pets are given private quiet corners, gentle lavender aromatherapy, and gradual introduction sessions with our calmest carers before joining any group activity.

Please bring your pet's regular food in pre-portioned bags, any daily medications with instructions, and their favorite blanket or small t-shirt with your scent to help them feel comfortable.

We understand that schedules change! We ask for at least 24 hours notice for grooming and walking appointments, and 48 hours notice for boarding stays to receive a full refund or free reschedule.
